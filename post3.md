# How to Speak
**By Dr. Anthony Onoja**

![How to Speak](https://donmaston09.github.io/CraftChronicles/assets/how_to_speak.png)

Do not deceive yourself: the fact that you can speak does not mean you know how to speak. Speech is common; mastery is rare. We are living in an age not of silence, but of excess—an era flooded with voices yet starved of meaning. Understand this early: speaking is not casual. It is power. It is structure. It is a test of awareness. Those who misuse it expose themselves; those who master it shape outcomes, command attention, and quietly position themselves ahead of others.

Human beings pride themselves on intelligence, yet what truly separates one man from another is not thought alone, but the ability to translate thought into precise language. Language is power organised into sound. It evolves across cultures and generations, yet it always serves one purpose: to reveal the mind of the speaker. From the beginning, man was given the ability to communicate what is invisible within him. Yet history gives a warning. At Babel, unity did not collapse because men lacked strength, but because their language was fractured. When speech breaks, alignment dies. When alignment dies, progress scatters. That pattern has not disappeared; it has simply taken a modern form.

### The Crisis of Expression

Look carefully at the world around you. The issue today is not ignorance, but disorder. There are individuals with sharp minds who remain unseen because they cannot express themselves with clarity. There are others, empty yet loud, who dominate spaces with noise mistaken for substance. In places such as Nigeria, this contradiction is particularly visible. Many do not lack intelligence; they lack structure. When opportunity appears, they fall into flattery, aggression, or silence—each one a quiet form of failure.

Do not misunderstand this truth: the world does not reward what you know; it rewards what you can articulate under pressure. Volume is not power. Silence is not weakness. Control is everything.

### The Age of Noise

The rise of social media has amplified confusion. Everyone speaks, yet few communicate. Noise has become currency, and substance has been abandoned. What many now celebrate as eloquence is often nothing more than decorated confusion—words arranged to impress but empty when examined. These are not communicators; they are performers. And the danger is this: many do not even realise they are failing.

> "The world is not listening for who speaks the most, but for who speaks with clarity."

### The Discipline of Speech

If you intend to speak with impact, you must first submit to discipline. Before you speak, do the work. Know your audience. Are they thinkers or observers, experts or beginners? Speaking without this awareness is not boldness; it is carelessness.

Write your thoughts before you speak them. Writing forces order. It exposes confusion. It reveals whether you truly understand your message or are merely hoping it will sound right. Those who hold influence—leaders, mentors, decision-makers—are not impressed by effort. They respond to clarity and structure, and they judge faster than you expect.

### Systems Over Emotion

You must learn to think in systems, not emotions. Every statement must have a source, a process, and an outcome. What are you saying? Why are you saying it? What should happen after you say it? If you cannot answer these questions, you are not speaking with intention—you are reacting.

Emotions are unstable. If you allow them to govern your speech, they will betray you. Learn restraint. Not every provocation deserves a response. Not every voice deserves your attention. There are those who thrive on drawing others into confusion. Refuse them.

Silence, when chosen deliberately, is not weakness; it is strategy.

### Clarity is Power

When you speak, remove unnecessary complexity. Avoid hiding behind jargon or inflated language. Clarity is not simplistic; it is precise. Speak so that your intention cannot be misunderstood. Let your words land exactly where you intend them to.

Carry yourself with balance. Not arrogance. Not timidity. Controlled confidence. Your presence speaks before your words ever do.

### The Five-Minute Test

If you cannot explain your vision, your value, and your direction in five minutes, then you do not yet understand them. Many fail here. They assume readiness until the moment demands clarity, and then they collapse into confusion.

Do not wait for that moment.

Practice. Refine your message. Remove ambiguity until your words become sharp, direct, and undeniable.

### The Verdict

Your tongue is not neutral. It is either building your future or dismantling it in real time. Every careless word leaks power. Every precise word builds it.

You are not judged by what you intend, but by what you express.

So choose differently. Train your speech. Discipline your mind. Refine your delivery.

And when you finally speak, do not merely be heard.

**Be impossible to ignore.**
