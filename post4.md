# The Spiritual Market Where Window Shopping Is Not Allowed
**By Dr. Anthony Onoja**

![Spiritual Market Artwork](https://donmaston09.github.io/CraftChronicles/assets/spiritual_market.png)

> “No man can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one and love the other; or else he will hold to the one and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and mammon.” — Matthew 6:24

Do not deceive yourself: the fact that you are alive does not mean you are neutral. Man is not merely flesh; he is spirit clothed in flesh. These two realities coexist, contend, and constantly demand alignment. Whether you acknowledge it or not, you are already involved in transactions beyond what your eyes can see.

Understand this early: there is a market. It is spiritual. It is active. And in this market, window shopping is not allowed.

### The Hunter’s Transaction

There once lived a hunter—precise, feared, and unusually successful. His traps did not fail. His arrows did not miss. While others returned empty-handed, he always came back with a catch. Soon, whispers spread: the man could hear the language of animals.

It was true.

On a certain day, while hunting, he unknowingly hid at a crossing point between realms—a thin veil between the visible and the unseen. As he waited, he saw spirits emerge. They retrieved a skin bag hidden within a termite mound. Each spirit waved it, and instantly, they vanished from human sight.

Curiosity led him where discipline should have stopped him.

He took the bag. He followed them. He entered what he was never invited into.

At the gathering, he sat among men but watched spirits mimic human behavior—laughing when men laughed, reacting as men reacted. But one spirit noticed him.

“This one sees us.”

Suspicion became confirmation. And when they discovered he had taken their instrument, they confronted him.

He bargained.

“I want to hear the language of animals.”

They agreed—but not without a condition: the day his secret was revealed, his life would be required.

He accepted power without fully weighing the cost.

Not long after, in what seemed like an ordinary moment, his secret was exposed. Pressed by his mother-in-law, he spoke. And the moment he spoke, the agreement was enforced.

He died.

Not because he was weak—but because he had entered a transaction he could not escape.

### The Market Is Real

This is why the story matters.

Because many are already trading in a market they do not understand.

The spiritual market is not symbolic. It is operational—precise, intelligent, and relentless. It does not require your awareness to function. It only requires your participation.

And hear this clearly:

**You are already a participant.**

There is no observer seat.  
No one stands at the edge.  
No one browses without consequence.  

You are either buying or selling.

### The Currency of the Unseen

This market does not trade in money.

Its currency is spiritual:
- Faith  
- Fear  
- Sacrifice  
- Prayer  
- Allegiance  

Its shelves are stocked with destinies, afflictions, delays, influence, bondage, clarity, confusion, life, and death.

Some enter consciously.  
Many enter through curiosity.  
Others are born into ongoing transactions tied to their bloodline.

But once engaged, something must be exchanged.

### Access Points

You ask: how do men enter a market that is not physical?

Through desire.  
Through curiosity.  
Through agreement—whether intentional or ignorant.

The doorway is not always a ritual. Sometimes it is a decision.

Scripture already warned you: *he who seeks finds.*

But it did not tell you what might be waiting when you arrive.

This market is mobile. It studies you. It tracks your patterns. It understands your weaknesses and presents tailored offers—crafted from your deepest desires and inherited tendencies.

What you call coincidence is often precision.

### The Illusion of Neutrality

Neutrality is not an option.

Even silence is a transaction.  
Even ignorance is participation.  
Even delay is a decision.  

Scripture reveals that Job was on earth while discussions about him were happening elsewhere. You think you are hidden—but your life is being interpreted beyond your awareness.

This market holds records—your history, your patterns, your tendencies. It builds offers around your weaknesses and advertises through personalized temptations.

What drives you determines what is given to you.

### The Three Instructions

If this is true, then what must you do?

#### 1. Choose Your Currency Deliberately

Faith or fear.  
Christ or mammon.  

There is no middle ground.

Faith is cultivated through discipline, prayer, and alignment with God. It is the force that allows a man to stand firm when everything around him collapses.

Fear, on the other hand, is deception. It presents illusions as reality and demands real payment for false perceptions.

Choose wisely.

#### 2. Confront Your Weakness

“Know thyself.”

Your greatest vulnerability is not external—it is internal. Your deepest desire, when left unchecked, becomes the negotiation point of your life.

Do not hide it.  
Do not justify it.  
Expose it.

What is concealed does not disappear—it matures.

Deal with greed, lust, anger, jealousy, hatred, and fear before they become the price you unknowingly pay.

#### 3. Run to the Cross

Do not attempt to outsmart this system.

You cannot win against a structure that predates you. This is not a game—it is a system of laws and consequences.

But there is an override.

Jesus paid the ultimate price.

Not partially.  
Not conditionally.  
Completely.  

The only true escape from endless transactions is total surrender.

### The Verdict

This market is active—with or without your awareness.

A transaction is already in progress.

So ask yourself:

What are you buying?  
What are you selling?  
And who truly owns your allegiance?

Because in this market—

**you will not leave empty-handed.**

## 🛡️ Final Charge

Do not delay what requires urgency.

Lay down your burdens. Surrender completely. Align yourself with truth while there is still time.

While you were yet a sinner, Christ died for you.

**Shalom.**
